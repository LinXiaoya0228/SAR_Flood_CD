### Mechanistic Findings from Telemetry:
1. **Equal $\lambda$ Does Not Mean Equal Gradient Strength:**
   - At $\lambda = 1.00$, the effective gradient ratio $\frac{\lambda \|g_{\text{anc}}\|_2}{\|g_{\text{cons}}\|_2}$ was $3.76$ for Features, $7.94$ for Probabilities, and **$141.21$** for Centered Logits.
   - The unbounded scale of unnormalized logits creates gradient magnitudes nearly two orders of magnitude larger than bounded probabilities or unit-sphere features.
2. **Partial Positive Alignment and Non-Collinearity:**
   - The mean cosine similarity $\cos(g_{\text{cons}}, g_{\text{anc}})$ remained consistently positive between $+0.25$ and $+0.51$ across all families. This demonstrates partial positive alignment rather than opposing gradient conflict (strictly reserved for negative cosine measurements).
   - However, the gradient directions exhibit substantial non-collinearity ($\theta \approx 60^\circ - 75^\circ$), indicating that the anchor component pulls along a distinct vector from the consistency adaptation trajectory rather than directly reinforcing it.
3. **Suppression of Adaptation vs. Risk Protection:**
   - In `E3_LOGIT_10`, where the anchor gradient dominated by $141\times$, the model update $\|\Delta \theta\|_2$ grew to $5.95 \times 10^{-4}$, but the adaptation simply clamped the model closer to the source predictions rather than selectively correcting errors.

## 6. The 13 Bounded Conclusions (Plan V3 Section 8)

As mandated by Plan V3 Section 8 and the Transition Audit, the following 13 bounded conclusions are formally established:

1. **Predefined Finite Weights Evaluated:** All three required source-anchor families (Probability KL, Centered Logit MSE, Feature Cosine L2) were successfully screened at the predefined finite weights $\{0.10, 0.50, 1.00\}$.
2. **Equal Numeric Lambda Is Not Equal Gradient Strength:** The effective weighted gradient ratio $\frac{\lambda \|g_{\text{anc}}\|_2}{\|g_{\text{cons}}\|_2}$ ranged from $0.38$ (Features, $\lambda=0.10$) to $141.21$ (Logits, $\lambda=1.00$). Numeric weights cannot be compared across loss families without empirical gradient normalization.
3. **Scalar Loss Ratio Is Not Gradient Ratio:** The scalar proportion of anchor loss does not predict its gradient impact on parameter updates due to differing loss curvatures and scale dynamics.
4. **Pre-Update Anchor Loss Dynamics:** Positive source-anchor loss exists before any parameter updates take place because of declared normalization-context differences (the frozen source model operates under running training statistics $\mu_{\text{train}}, \sigma^2_{\text{train}}$ in `eval()` mode, while the candidate operates under target test-batch statistics $\mu_{\text{batch}}, \sigma^2_{\text{batch}}$ in `train()` mode) and/or weak-versus-strong view differences. It must not be attributed exclusively to running-statistic divergence.
5. **Source Agreement Is NOT Correctness:** High agreement with frozen source outputs does not imply correct change detection under novel environmental distributions.
6. **Approaching Source Predictions Is Insufficient Evidence of Safety:** Clamping candidate outputs toward the source baseline suppresses both harmful and beneficial adaptations indiscriminately.
7. **Aggregate F1 Changes May Reflect Adaptation Suppression:** Marginal metric variations under strong anchors result primarily from damping test-time parameter movement rather than intelligent selective risk mitigation.
8. **Beneficial Adaptation Losses Must Be Reported Alongside Recovered Harm:** In `E3_LOGIT_10`, recovering 13.90% of base errors cost a 10.98% destruction of beneficial adaptations, yielding near-zero net transfer benefit.
9. **Promotion Rule Evaluated Strictly on Unrounded Metrics:** The formal promotion rule was evaluated without rounding: $(\Delta \text{Macro} \ge +0.01) \lor (\Delta \text{Worst} > 0 \land \Delta \text{Macro} \ge 0.0)$.
10. **All Nine Cells Failed Promotion:** Maximum $\Delta \text{Macro}$ was $+0.000502$ ($< +0.010000$) and $\Delta \text{WorstEvent}$ was $+0.000000$ ($> 0.000000$). The formal verdict is **`NONE RETAINED`**.
11. **Standalone Anchoring Is Ineffective for Catastrophic Harm Prevention:** Across all nine cells, standalone source anchoring failed to rescue the worst event (Event 1111003) and failed to achieve the +1.0 pp macro promotion threshold.
12. **Seed 1 / Order B Repeat Experiments Are NOT REQUIRED:** Under Plan V3 Section 8.3 and Section 1.3, repeat experiments are triggered only for promoted candidate recipes. Because the screening verdict is `NONE RETAINED`, no repeat GPU runs are required or permitted.
13. **Block E3 Is Terminally Complete; Proceed to Block E4:** Block E3 is formally closed via the approved negative-result route. The research investigation proceeds directly to **Block E4: Class-Aware Intervention Experiments**.
